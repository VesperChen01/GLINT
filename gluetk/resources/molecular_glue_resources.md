## 📖 Key Publications

### Foundational Papers
1. **Thalidomide mechanism** - Fischer ES et al. (2014) Nature. "Structure of the DDB1-CRBN E3 ubiquitin ligase in complex with thalidomide"
2. **IMiD neo-substrates** - Krönke J et al. (2014) Science. "Lenalidomide causes selective degradation of IKZF1 and IKZF3"
3. **GSPT1 degradation** - Matyskiela ME et al. (2016) Nature. "A novel cereblon modulator recruits GSPT1 to the CRL4CRBN ubiquitin ligase"
4. **Indisulam mechanism** - Han T et al. (2017) Science. "Anticancer sulfonamides target splicing by inducing RBM39 degradation"

### Reviews
1. **Molecular glue review** - Kozicka Z & Thomä NH (2021) Cell Chem Biol. "Haven't got a glue: Protein surface variation for the design of molecular glue degraders"
2. **TPD landscape** - Bekes M et al. (2022) Nat Rev Drug Discov. "PROTAC targeted protein degraders: the past is prologue"
3. **E3 ligase hijacking** - Schapira M et al. (2019) Nat Rev Drug Discov. "Targeted protein degradation: expanding the toolbox"

### Computational Methods
1. **Ternary complex modeling** - Zaidman D et al. (2020) J Chem Inf Model. "PRosettaC: Rosetta Based Modeling of PROTAC Mediated Ternary Complexes"
2. **Cooperativity prediction** - Drummond ML & Williams CI (2019) J Chem Inf Model. "In Silico Modeling of PROTAC-Mediated Ternary Complexes"

## 🔗 Quick Links for GlueTK Users

### Fetch Example Structures in PyMOL
```python
# CRBN-Thalidomide
fetch 4CI1

# CRBN-Lenalidomide-CK1α
fetch 6H0F

# CRBN-CC885-GSPT1
fetch 6H0G

# DCAF15-Indisulam-RBM39
fetch 5S9M
```

### GlueTK Quick Commands
```python
# G-motif detection
find_crbn_g_motif('structure_name', rmsd_cutoff=3.5)

# PPI interface analysis
ppi_analyze('complex', ['A'], ['B'])

# Neo-epitope identification
neo_epitope_find('complex', ['A'], ['B'], 'LIG')
```
